Version 1.5.2 fixes one problem on the first-run setup screen of the desktop app. Nothing else changed.

## Fixed

- **"Set up later" and "Start collecting" did not leave the setup screen.** On the very first start, the screen that asks which system logs to collect saved your choice but then showed itself again, so it looked as if nothing had happened. Only closing and reopening the app got you past it. The choice is now accepted at once and you land on the Overview. This affected every fresh install, including the upgrade from 1.4.0 and earlier on a new computer; it did not lose any data.

## Good to know

- If you are stuck on that screen in 1.5.0 or 1.5.1, closing and reopening the app also works; your choice was already saved.
- Everything listed under "Good to know" in the 1.5.0 and 1.5.1 notes still applies: the new screens are English only, the installer is unsigned, and sign-in with roles, email, Slack and Discord notifications, journald and macOS collection have not been tried against real services or machines.

## Upgrading

Install over the existing version; your data is kept.
