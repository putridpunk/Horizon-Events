Version 1.5.3 translates the screens added in 1.5.0 and fixes a few small problems found while testing them.

## Changed

- **The new screens are now translated.** Sign-in and users, the account and update cards, detection rules (editor, test and replay, Sigma import), notifications, scheduled reports, owner and tags, threat lists, the syslog receiver, the location database, the customizable Overview, and event search with saved searches and exports now appear in all twelve languages. Sentences that contain names or numbers are translated as a whole.
- **Detection rule HZ-016 (Suspicious PowerShell)** no longer fires on `FromBase64String` alone, which many harmless scripts use. Encoded commands, `Invoke-Expression`, downloads and the other patterns still trigger it, and you can add the word back in the rule's settings.

## Fixed

- **Settings no longer shows a red error for people who are not administrators.** The location database, notification and scheduled report cards are hidden for them instead of failing.
- **Scheduled reports show the real date and time of the next run** instead of "just now".
- **The email channel list in scheduled reports refreshes** by itself, so a channel you just added appears without reloading.
- **An alert's history no longer says "OPEN → OPEN"** when only the owner or tags changed; it says "Updated".

## Good to know

- Slack and Discord notifications, signed installers, the macOS collector and sign-in with real Windows accounts have still not been tried against real services or machines.
- Names that contain a person's name in screen reader labels are not translated yet.

## Upgrading

Install over the existing version; your data is kept.
